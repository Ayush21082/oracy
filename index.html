Communication, articulation, storytelling and speaking will be the most important skills to have in the post-AI world.

Just think about it. What will make humans valuable after AI can do all the work humans do? And the answer is your ability to connect with other people. Because that’s the one thing AI cannot do. AI cannot build real, authentic, genuine human connections.

And most of us are not naturally good communicators. It’s extremely rare that someone just grows up as a good communicator without putting in any effort to get there.

Straight answers. Tap a question.

Do I need to be a good speaker already?

No. Most of us are not naturally good communicators. Oracy is for getting there, one minute at a time.

How long does it take?

One minute of speaking. Then your feedback is ready to read.

What does the AI actually tell me?

Scores for fluency, grammar, vocabulary, clarity and confidence, notes on your pace and filler words, one thing to improve, and a better way to phrase something you said.

What happens to my recordings?

Your audio is analyzed to score how you spoke. It is not used to train AI models, and you can delete your recordings anytime.

Is Oracy free?

You get 3 free sessions every week. Oracy Pro gives you unlimited practice and full feedback every session, with a 7-day free trial.
